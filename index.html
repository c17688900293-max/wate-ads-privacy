隐私声明（中文）

本隐私声明适用于由深圳市蛙特科技有限公司运营的 “WATE Amazon Ads MCP”（以下简称“本应用”）。本应用用于连接经授权的 Amazon Ads 账户，并生成内部广告报表、趋势分析、异常监控及优化建议。

1. 我们访问的数据

在账户所有者明确授权后，本应用可能通过 Amazon Ads API 或 Amazon 官方 MCP 服务访问：

  • 广告主、账户及广告资料（profile）标识；
  • 广告活动、广告组、关键词、商品投放、搜索词及版位信息；
  • 展示量、点击量、花费、订单、归因销售额及相关广告绩效指标；
  • 完成 OAuth 授权与 API 连接所必需的访问令牌和技术日志。

本应用不要求或存储您的 Amazon 登录密码，不收集支付卡信息，也不以本应用功能为目的收集无关的消费者个人信息。

2. 数据用途

  • 制作广告表现报表并进行趋势、搜索词、版位和预算分析；
  • 识别异常消耗、低效投放及潜在优化机会；
  • 维护授权连接、排查技术故障并保障服务安全；
  • 履行适用法律、Amazon Ads API 政策及其他合规义务。

我们不会出售或出租通过本应用获取的数据，也不会将这些数据用于与授权目的无关的第三方广告营销。

3. 数据共享与安全

数据仅在实现上述目的所必需的范围内，由获得授权的内部人员、Amazon 相关服务以及必要的技术基础设施处理。除非法律要求或为保护合法权利所必需，我们不会向其他第三方披露。

我们采用 HTTPS 传输、访问权限控制、凭证加密或受控保管、日志审查及最小权限原则等合理措施保护数据。任何互联网传输或存储方式均无法保证绝对安全，但我们会持续评估并改进安全控制。

4. 数据保存与您的选择

我们仅在实现授权用途、维持服务或履行法律义务所需期间保存数据。当授权被撤销、业务目的结束或收到有效删除请求时，我们将依照适用要求删除或匿名化相关数据。

您可以随时在 Amazon 账户的授权管理设置中撤销本应用的访问权限。撤销后，本应用将无法继续获取新的 Amazon Ads 数据。

Privacy Notice (English)

This Privacy Notice applies to “WATE Amazon Ads MCP” (the “Application”), operated by 深圳市蛙特科技有限公司. The Application connects to authorized Amazon Ads accounts to provide internal reporting, trend analysis, anomaly monitoring, and optimization recommendations.

1. Data We Access

After explicit authorization by the account owner, the Application may access the following through the Amazon Ads API or an official Amazon MCP service:

  • advertiser, account, and advertising profile identifiers;
  • campaign, ad group, keyword, product-targeting, search-term, and placement information;
  • impressions, clicks, spend, orders, attributed sales, and related advertising performance metrics; and
  • access tokens and technical logs necessary for OAuth authorization and API connectivity.

The Application does not request or store your Amazon password, payment card information, or unrelated consumer personal information.

2. How We Use Data

  • to generate advertising performance reports and analyze trends, search terms, placements, and budgets;
  • to identify unusual spend, inefficient targeting, and potential optimization opportunities;
  • to maintain authorized connections, troubleshoot technical issues, and protect service security; and
  • to comply with applicable law, Amazon Ads API policies, and other compliance obligations.

We do not sell or rent data obtained through the Application, and we do not use it for unrelated third-party advertising.

3. Sharing and Security

Data is processed only as necessary for the purposes above by authorized internal personnel, applicable Amazon services, and required technical infrastructure. We do not disclose it to other third parties unless required by law or necessary to protect legitimate rights.

We use reasonable safeguards including HTTPS transmission, access controls, protected credential storage, log review, and least-privilege practices. No transmission or storage method is completely secure, but we continually review and improve our controls.

4. Retention and Your Choices

We retain data only for as long as needed to provide the authorized service or meet legal obligations. When authorization is withdrawn, the business purpose ends, or a valid deletion request is received, we delete or anonymize relevant data in accordance with applicable requirements.

You may revoke the Application’s access at any time through your Amazon account authorization settings. After revocation, the Application will no longer receive new Amazon Ads data.
